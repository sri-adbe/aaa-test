Frequently Asked Questions

What discounts are available through AAA Discounts & Rewards? +

AAA members can enjoy savings on thousands of offers from national and local partners. Discounts are available on shopping, gas, dining, travel, entertainment, attractions, museums, zoos, ski destinations, and more. The AAA Discounts & Rewards program helps members save on everyday purchases as well as special experiences throughout the year.

Does AAA offer discounted tickets to attractions and theme parks? +

Yes. AAA members can access discounted tickets and special offers for popular attractions, theme parks, museums, zoos, aquariums, water parks, sporting events and seasonal destinations. Members can also receive Preferred AccessTM tickets for the most high-demand tickets for concerts, sports and major events around the world.

Does AAA offer discounts on ski tickets and other seasonal activities? +

AAA members can find seasonal savings throughout the year, including discounts on ski lift tickets, ski passes, family attractions, and regional destinations. Available offers vary by season and location, so members should check current deals regularly for the latest savings opportunities.
